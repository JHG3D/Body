Body · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026
App: Body (com.body_strength)

Summary

Body stores your workout data on your device only. We do not operate user accounts or collect workout data on our servers.

Data stored on your device

  • Workout sessions (date, exercise track, level, sets, reps, progression metadata)
  • App settings (sound, theme, weekly schedule, training reminder preferences)
  • Local profile names (no email or login)

Data stays on your device. There is no cloud backup or import in this version.

Data we do not collect

  • No account system or cloud sync operated by us
  • No analytics server operated by us for this app version
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone

Sharing with third parties

  • Google Play processes app installs and, when available, in-app purchases under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Body may show Google AdMob full-screen ads (interstitials) for free users after a training session is saved. Body Pro is ad-free when unlocked. AdMob may process device identifiers under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Body Pro

Premium features may be unlocked via a one-time Google Play in-app purchase when that feature is enabled. Purchase data is handled by Google Play.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general fitness use. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Questions: support@body.app
